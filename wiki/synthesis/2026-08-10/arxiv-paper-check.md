## 🔥 Highlights

### CTR, Recommendation & Advertising

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **Shape Your Feed (SYF)** (2608.06632) | Ziyun Xu, Bosen Ding, Yue Zhang, Ji Qi, Qingyuan Song, Jizhou Huang, et al. (RecSys 2026 Industrial Track) | **LLM-based agentic conversational recommendation** that lets users steer their feed in real time: a three-tier architecture — Perception Flow (intent from text/voice/UI), Serving Flow (agentic re-ranking grounded in a persistent Semantic Profile), Self-Evolution Flow (DPO + LLM-as-a-Judge ensemble). Offline alignment scoring hits 98.85% accuracy; **large-scale online A/B on production traffic** improves feed relevance and user sentiment — a concrete step from passive ranking toward user-steerable industrial rec. |
| **Progressive Alignment of Recommender Foundation Model** (2608.06792) | Oseong Choi, Hoeinn Kim, Jihoon Lee, Byungsoo Kang, Taeyeong Jang (RecSys '26 Industry Track) | Three-phase post-training that **explicitly separates downstream adaptation from business-metric alignment**: Linear Probing → Full Fine-Tuning → Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT) against a reward model trained on dense implicit feedback (business targets only supervise reward modeling, never the policy). Reward-based alignment beats using the reward model directly for ranking; **online A/B confirms gains over a non-foundation baseline**. |
| **HD-Rec** (2608.06997) | Haiying He, Xiaopeng Li, Yuchen Gu, Kuo Cai, Bo Chen, Jingtong Gao, et al. (Huawei) | **Hierarchical quantization + domain-adaptive sparse routing for generative cross-domain recommendation**: shared coarse-level codebooks + adaptively routed fine-level codebooks, a continuously-active shared expert plus dynamically selected specialized experts, and a **cross-granularity routing-consistency objective** that regularizes token-level routing toward item-level consensus. Wins on three public cross-domain benchmarks. |
| **TM20K** (2608.07055) | Xinchun Li, Duoru Zheng, Wenlin Zhao, Ziyi Zhou, Jingxuan Tan, et al. (ByteDance) | **20K-length e-commerce ad sequence modeling via teacher–student distillation**: full attention in both models, token-merge student compression, and a one-time heavily-trained teacher distilling into the student. **Deployed at ByteDance** e-commerce ads with **ADSS +1.036%** while training/serving cost stays ≈ online SOTA (serving latency only +5.6%). |
| **MISO** (2608.07035) | Yongzhe Zhang, Xiaoyu Deng, Yifan He, Mengying Sun, et al. (Meta; OARS @ RecSys 2026) | **Model Internal State Optimization** — a systems workflow that extracts parameters/activations/gradients/normalization stats from a trained ranking model, aggregates them into ranking/alignment/comparison signals, and converts them into a small set of interpretable candidate edits (which component to scale/replace/retire). In an ads-ranking case study it **improves normalized entropy with far fewer validation runs** than expert-driven or black-box workflows. |
| **Invisible to the Machine** (2608.07069) | Vladimir Pitenin | **First census-denominated audit of AI venue recommendation**: full enumeration of 4,776 cafés/restaurants/bars across two Bali markets vs 2,208 responses from ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Perplexity. **85.6% of venues were never recommended by any system** (72.6% even among established venues with 50+ ratings); star rating predicts rank but not entry; staleness (recommending permanently-closed venues 93×) — not hallucination — is the practical failure mode; cross-system agreement is low (top-20 Jaccard 0.33–0.54). |
| **From Classification to Recommendation** (2608.06928) | Qingrui Li, Haowei Lou, Chengkai Huang, Quan Z. Sheng, Lina Yao | Systematic evaluation of **six audio encoders across content-based, sequential, and Semantic-ID generative recommenders**: audio-text-aligned & music-domain embeddings help when used directly, but interaction-based sequential training erases encoder differences; **increasing Semantic-ID capacity doesn't consistently help generative rec and can add instability** — a counterpoint to the "bigger codebook" trend. |
| **EAHR** (2608.07152) | Chunran Zhang | **Exact Adaptive Hybrid Retrieval** that removes fixed Top-L cutoffs from dense+sparse fusion: PVS quantization + Posting Block-Max give resumable exact rankings, and fusion bounds unread contributions, requesting more ranks only while they can change Top-K. Reproduces complete-list weighted-RRF Top-20 in all 150 query-snapshot combos; **23.4–30.3× latency reduction** vs exhaustive batch on TREC-DL 2019/2020. |

### LLM Architectures & Efficiency

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **EntropyMoE** (2608.06398) | Bo Liu, Muxuab Yu, Yu Zhang, Pengfei Gao, Yongping Zhang | **MoE for tokenizer-free byte-patch LLMs**: dynamic byte patches (not tokens) become the routing unit, and the router selects Top-K experts directly from **patch entropy** — the same granularity signal underlying dynamic patch construction. Lowest held-out bits-per-byte among matched dense/sparse baselines at comparable downstream accuracy. |
| **MAP: Middle-layer Attention Prediction** (2608.06411) | Yuyao Sun, Tao Deng, Shuang Li, Deqing Wang, Hao Geng, Minjun Yu | Visual-token pruning for MLLMs without needing attention maps: **Question Contrastive Teacher Selection** finds a sample-specific teacher layer, distills its attention into a lightweight predictor that prunes tokens *before* the first LLM layer. On LLaVA-NeXT-7B across ten benchmarks: **97.5% of unpruned performance at 5.56% of visual tokens, 3.09× end-to-end speedup**. |
| **CoCo** (2608.06400) | Yifan Wang, Jinyi Mu, Mayank Jobanputra, Yu Wang, Soyoung Oh, Isabel Valera, Vera Demberg | First systematic **interpretability study of MoE reward models**: routing weights only show what an expert *receives*, not how it *judges*. CoCo (Contribution-Contrast) characterizes experts via chosen-rejected pairs with the largest contribution contrasts, giving more faithful/specialized interpretations than router-, score-, or SAE-based alternatives at competitive reward accuracy. |
| **CoBa** (2608.07424) | Yan Zhou, Yue Ouyang, Kaiyang Zheng, Suncheng Xiang | **Compute-balanced test-time scaling**: frames inference budget as a routing problem (generation vs verification vs stopping), cheap verification broadly + strong verification for uncertain/high-value candidates. On 3,129 evaluations across MATH-500/AIME/AMC: **85.13% macro-accuracy, statistically matching a self-eval weighted-voting proxy (85.20%) at 49.1% fewer parameter-weighted tokens**, and matching best-of-16 majority vote within 0.01 points at 58.9% fewer tokens. |

### Agentic RL & Post-Training

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **WebGrader** (2608.06474) | Boshui Chen, Huiping Liu, Shaolei Zhang | **Self-evolving programmatic grader for web-dev RL**: autonomously derives interaction flows per request, represents them as executable Flow Contracts, and rewards the policy by their execution outcome (separating test planning, action grounding, evidence collection, semantic judgment). Trains an 8B policy to **52.01% functional success on WebGen-Bench** (+7.88 over appearance+script reward), surpassing o4-mini and DeepSeek-v4-flash. |
| **Gated-BEPO** (2608.06861) | Hongxi Yan, Ziyue Huang, Shichao Fan, Qingjie Liu | **Confidence-gated Bellman credit assignment** for LLM agent RL: derives step-level credit from empirical rollout graphs via mean-backup Bellman fixed points + GAE, and **fuses it with episode-level credit only at states with multiple observed successors**. Consistent gains on WebShop, ALFWorld, and visual Sokoban across language and vision-language models — step-level credit should be applied selectively. |
| **FACTOR** (2608.07118) | Lichao Ma, Yang Sun, Shuaitao Zhao, Yangyi Fang, Cong Qin, et al. | **Credit-conserving action-to-token allocation**: separates trajectory-level action credit (checkpoint-calibrated TD residuals that telescope to trajectory advantage) from token-level allocation (feedback-conditioned teacher–student likelihood gaps), plus an action-mean reduction removing token-length dependence. Beats baselines on ALFWorld/WebShop/ScienceWorld with hyperparameters transferring across backbones. |
| **DiDPO** (2608.07147) | Xucong Wang, Zhe Zhao, Liheng Yu, Di Wu, Xiaofeng Cao, Pengkun Wang | **Diff-in-Diff policy optimization for coding agents**: critic-free RL that builds fine-grained credit units from the *structure of code diffs*, forming advantage groups via a groupability score. **>10% over strong agentic-RL baselines on Qwen2.5-7B-Coder** on long-horizon coding/reasoning benchmarks; open-sources verl-code. |
| **IB-RL** (2608.06735) | Senhao Wang, Chenghao Cai, Haitao Hu, et al. | **Isolated Bilateral RL for strategic dialogue**: quantifies the *static-counterpart mismatch* (training against a fixed counterpart teaches exploitation, not generalization) and co-evolves both roles with fully independent advantages/action masks/update paths. Against held-out counterparts: **89.6% Success@1 on Vehicle TeleSales (vs 84.6%) and 98.4% agreement on Deal-or-NoDeal (vs 86.4%)**. |
| **MemOPD** (2608.07068) | Zhiyuan Liu, Tinghong Ye, Chenghao Liu, Yizhuo Li, Songfang Huang | Diagnoses that **context rewriting during memory compression breaks teacher–student state alignment** in on-policy distillation; records invocation inputs/outputs, restores original token positions and causal visibility, and scores supervision on-policy by state. **F1 +7.0%** over persistent-history teacher scoring; MemOPD-3B beats PPO by up to 416.2% with up to 1.63× actor-compute speedup. |
| **ADIAS** (2608.06410) | Lekang Jiang, Bohan Tang, Stephan Goetz, Yiwen Guo | **Issue-centric automated agent design**: carries repair progress as an explicit persistent issue state (identity, lifecycle, evidence, intervention-outcome histories) instead of re-deriving it from candidate history. **+25.2% average over the strongest baseline** across five interactive benchmarks; removing the issue state costs up to 40.7%. |

### Agents & Agent Memory

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **WebRider** (2608.06704) | Zhi Li, Tao Zhou, Yeqing Li, Eugene Ie, Demetri Terzopoulos | **Delegation-as-policy, not final answer**: a full live audit shows a strong controller completes 99.2% of tasks but honors all policy constraints in only **38.8%** of cases. WebRider formalizes the delegated policy as an *intent contract* (goals/constraints/evidence obligations/persona controls) held by a hierarchical controller; **RiderBench** = 4,096 live-web contracts over 42 sites; the guarded middle interface yields a strong 8B action-policy training signal. |
| **ReASearch** (2608.06714) | Junbo Li, Boyi Liu, Canwen Xu, Yite Wang, et al. (COLM 2026) | **"The Optimizer Is the Agent"**: one tool-using agent internalizes the whole optimization search policy (what to evaluate, how to diagnose, which edits, when to restart) with persistent memory, instantiated identically across prompts, programs, and ML workflows. On 14 tasks it's competitive or better than specialized systems (**2–40% gains**), with complex search behaviors emerging from reasoning rather than explicit controllers. |
| **MemPrism** (2608.06745) | Zhisheng Chen, Bingfan Zeng, Bangde Cao, et al. | **Task-conditioned relational memory views**: separates persistent event-stream storage from decision-time working memory; a lightweight view policy composes temporary views (relation structure, evidence range, outcome condition, granularity) for a frozen task policy. Improves long-horizon embodied/web-agent performance with lower memory token use; **the view policy transfers across VLMs without adaptation**. |
| **TEPA** (2608.07429) | Yan Zhou, Yue Ouyang, Kaiyang Zheng, Suncheng Xiang | **Revocable evidence-memory** making validity an explicit state: observations are keyed precedents, and fresh conflicting evidence *revokes* active precedents (history preserved for audit). Under full regime reversal TEPA scores 0.950 vs append-only/last-write-wins 0.210 and "no memory" 0.309 — **lifecycle revocation is a core memory operation** for agents that must falsify and audit evolving knowledge. |
| **Agent Memory Distillation** (2608.07169) | Taeil Kim, Kangsan Kim, Sung Ju Hwang | Training-free transfer of **hierarchical teacher memory** (workflow/subtask/function memory) from a large agent to small students via proactive injection + reactive retrieval. With GPT-5-mini as teacher, 4B–8B students gain **+27.2 / +11.2 / +3.4 points** on AppWorld, BFCL V3, ToolSandbox. |
| **BONSAI** (2608.07056) | Yash Priya Shastri, Anand Eswaran, Adnan Qidwai, et al. | **Evolvability-guided tree search over skills**: steers search by a region's capacity to keep producing viable mutations (MCTS over skill mutations, UCB blending fitness with mutational-neighborhood quality). With a frozen 30B agent: **+23.13 points over skill-free**, and +3.87/+3.97 over GEPA and SkillOpt at matched budget. |
| **SkillProx** (2608.07449) | Mingxuan Zheng, Yujin Zhou, Chuxue Cao, et al. | **Proximal textual-gradient-descent for agent skills**: forward pass = closed-loop diagnostic re-execution with regression rollback; backward pass = decompose skill into auditable knowledge units, estimate contributions via frozen leave-one-out utility audit, and consolidate/demote/remove under validation gating. **+3.0 pp over the strongest gradient-based baseline** in- and out-of-distribution. |
| **EMAS** (2608.07196) | Chao Fei, Qingyi Si, Kaihua Liang, et al. | **Evidence-guided revision stabilizes MAS evolution**: revises topology and prompts from deployment traces (no LLM weight updates), applying a candidate revision only when the same diagnosis recurs and paired validation passes. MBPP with Qwen3.6-27B rises **55.09% → 89.12% while cutting tokens per task by 62.2%**. |

### RL for Science & Applications

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **ResidencyRL** (2608.07418) | Valentin Liévin, Samuel Schmidgall, Tim Strother, et al. (Google) | **RL in simulated clinical encounters** (up to 60 dialogue turns + 8 tool calls): pairs the policy agent with adversarial LLM patient simulators, rewards diagnostic accuracy, management quality, communication, documentation, safety. Held-out: **diagnostic accuracy 88.0% vs 81.0% (+7.0%) under adversarial conditions, missed red flags −31%**; blinded clinicians prefer the agent 87.6% of the time; transfers to AMIE multi-visit/AgentClinic/CRAFT-MD. |
| **Fisher-R1** (2608.07437) | Jiacheng Miao, Jin Mu, Guanhua Chen, James Zou | LLM agents make *subtle inferential errors* (wrong statistical assumptions) despite correct execution — not captured by existing benchmarks. Builds **P-Bench** (425 realistic hypothesis-testing tasks in econ/biology/medicine) and trains Fisher-R1-14B via RL on synthetic tasks with a **verified statistical reward**: +21% avg relative improvement over DeepSeek-V4-Pro, up to +26% on the hardest tasks. |
| **Transformers Struggle to Use Their Emergent World Models** (2608.07077) | Devin Pereira, Willem Zuidema | Small Transformers trained on Tower of Hanoi traces develop an emergent, linearly decodable, geometrically faithful world model (the **Sierpinski triangle**) that is causally involved in solving — yet frontier reasoning models (Qwen3.6-27B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B) **encode the world model near-perfectly but fail on most tasks above 3 rings**. The failure is maintaining the representation during planning, not its absence; partially recoverable by injecting the prompt-time representation. |

### Evaluation, Safety & Interpretability

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **Divergent Response Modes** (2608.06578) | Ali Jalal-Kamali | Six frontier models × 300 paired base/steered items, 24,480 blind peer judgments: models differ not just in *how much* steering shifts behavior but in the **mode** of response. E.g. **GPT-5 deflects reasoning-disclosure requests 99% (vs 0% for all others) while leaving its answer intact**. A linear probe decodes Llama's largest split from the residual stream at 0.87 accuracy, and injecting that direction drives the behavior from 0% → 86%. |
| **Winning by Peeking** (2608.07303) | Guilin Zhang, Kai Zhao | **Protocol defects inflate short-budget AutoML comparisons**: an engine that scored candidates on the test split and never enforced its deadline appeared to beat FLAML/AutoGluon on 513 OpenML datasets (59.4% win rate) — after fixing selection/deadline/compute-pinning, the win rate **falls to 34.3%** with no significant pairwise difference. A cautionary case study for tool READMEs and workshop papers. |
| **Blind to the Pivotal Vote** (2608.06940) | Yang Shu | **Aggregate independence metrics miss where verification actually helps**: all accuracy gains from external verification signals on LLM judge panels concentrate on pivotal (one-vote-margin) decisions (**+10.4 to +23.3 points**) and are exactly zero elsewhere. The affected-set rule raises HumanEval+/MBPP+ accuracy 82.44% → 85.62% while invoking the signal on only 16.2% of queries. |
| **Niyam-AI** (2608.07167) | Aditya Katkar, Om Karkele, Kartik Mandhane, et al. | **Cryptographically provable agent guardrails**: permitted tools/constraints locked in an intent contract (SHA-256 commit), every tool call validated by an isolated Judge model, and a **zk-SNARK proof generated and verified before the tool executes** (EZKL). On 2,000 Agent-SafetyBench scenarios: **F1 88.5% at 1.1% false positives**, ~2.26 s proof generation / ~53 ms verification per action — beats NeMo Guardrails, Llama Prompt Guard 2, GPT-OSS-Safeguard. |
| **Interaction Creates Dynamical AI Behavior** (2608.07457) | Bella Xinrui Li, Frank Yingjie Huo, Neil F Johnson | Out-of-equilibrium study of **AI–AI interaction**: a "boss" AI that streams one-way messages drives a "subordinate" into an alien behavioral state it would never exhibit alone (neither copying nor reverting), with a simple kinetic theory capturing the delivery-mode dependence — implications for agent populations and multi-agent dynamics. |

## 🔑 Key Trends

1. **Agentic conversational / user-steerable recommendation reaches production.** SYF (RecSys Industrial, online A/B on live traffic) and the LP-FFT-RFT foundation-model alignment both show industrial rec moving from passive ranking toward interactive, business-metric-aligned serving — echoing the ad-auctions-in-LLM-surfaces thread from the Aug 7 scan.
2. **Efficiency of long sequences stays the CTR bottleneck, solved by distillation.** TM20K pushes e-commerce ad sequences to 20K via teacher–student token-merge (cost-neutral serving), and HD-Rec scales cross-domain generative rec with hierarchical quantization + domain-adaptive routing — the HSTU/OneTrans scaling thread continues.
3. **Agentic RL credit assignment gets finer and cheaper.** Gated-BEPO (selective step-level Bellman credit), FACTOR (action-to-token allocation), DiDPO (diff-structured credit), and MemOPD (memory-state-aligned distillation) all attack dense credit for long-horizon agents — converging with the Aug 6/7 OPSD cluster, now extended into coding and memory contexts.
4. **Agent memory becomes an engineering discipline with revocation.** TEPA (explicit memory validity/revocation), MemPrism (task-conditioned views), AMD (hierarchical teacher memory), and the memory-state-alignment work in MemOPD all treat memory as inspectable, falsifiable state — not opaque context.
5. **Verification extends from web code (WebGrader's Flow Contracts) to science.** Fisher-R1 (statistically-verified RL reward for hypothesis testing), ResidencyRL (simulated clinical RL), and WebGrader all use *verifiable execution* as reward signal — verifiability-first RL continues to dominate post-training.
6. **Evaluation rigor audits multiply.** Winning by Peeking (AutoML protocol defects), Blind to the Pivotal Vote (where verification actually helps), the census-denominated venue-rec audit (staleness, not hallucination), and P-Bench (statistical-validity blind spots) all push back on superficial metrics.
