## 🔥 Highlights

### CTR, Recommendation, Advertising & IR

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **Sona** (2608.11015) | Sona Team — Alexandr Udeneev, Aleksei Krasilnikov, Alexey Nadtochiy, et al. (32 authors; Yandex Music) | **Single-model generative recommender that replaced the entire production cascade at Yandex Music** (15+ candidate generators + pre-rankers + rankers consuming hundreds of features, incl. transformer signals like Argus). Encoder turns the user's logged engagement sequence into hidden states shared by an autoregressive decoder and a Ranking Module; next-token-prediction + distillation objectives couple generation and ranking; the Teacher Ranker supplies targets at training and is **absent from serving**. Online A/B on "My Vibe" smart-speaker traffic: **+4.53% Active Users** (primary), **+6.30% Total Listening Time**, **+11.42% Likes** — the Active-Users uplift is 2.35× that previously delivered by Argus. |
| **From Prediction to Incrementality** (2608.10182) | Changshuai Wei, John Bencina, Phuc Nguyen, Andre Assuncao Silva T Ribeiro, Benjamin Zelditch (LinkedIn) | **Decision-centric causal optimization for targeting/recommendation**: instead of predictive scores + heuristic allocation (which misallocates toward users who would act anyway), a single-objective framework aligns a causal neural network (Transformer backbone) for individual treatment-effect estimation, a Bayesian neural-bandit layer for uncertainty-aware exploration, and a dual-based large-scale LP layer for constrained allocation. Supports sequential context and multi-outcome attribute-conditioned scoring. Online A/B on **LinkedIn Feed marketing traffic: +7.20% lift** in the primary long-term-value metric; distills production lessons on causal training-data construction and cost/delivery control. |
| **Do LLM Recommenders Know When They're Hallucinating?** (2608.10008) | Srijith Ravikumar | **First audit of confidence calibration in catalog faithfulness of LLM recommenders** (4 zero-shot vendors — Mistral Large, Llama-3.3-70B, GPT-OSS-120B, Claude Sonnet 4.6 — over 3 catalogs, 12 cells). OOD@10 hallucination is catalog-dependent (0–0.2% MovieLens / 4.5–8.3% Amazon / 2.2–8.4% Yelp), but verbalized confidence is **systematically under-confident everywhere** (mean 67–86 on items recommended with 92–100% accuracy — the opposite of usual over-confidence), with ECE up to 0.223 even at 0% hallucination. Reads this as an **elicitation mismatch**: "Just Ask" prompts elicit generic quality ratings, not catalog-membership probabilities. A conformal abstention threshold reduces hallucination by at most 0.7 pp at 4–21 pp coverage cost — the under-confident channel cannot separate correct items from hallucinations. Recommends catalog-anchored elicitation + reporting calibration alongside OOD. |
| **Are We Really Making Progress in Group Recommendation?** (2608.11190; *RecSys 2026*) | Song-Duo Ma, Pu-Jen Cheng | **Systematic evaluation-bias exposé — "the tie-breaking illusion"**: an extra sigmoid before the BPR objective inflates tied top scores, making HR@K/NDCG@K highly sensitive to deterministic tie-breaking. Re-evaluating recent group-recommendation methods under a tie-aware protocol (exact expectation of HR@K/NDCG@K under uniform random tie-breaking), **many reported improvements shrink substantially and relative rankings change markedly** on CAMRa2011 and Mafengwo. Temperature-scaled BPR keeps the benefit without tie inflation. A methodological warning with wide applicability to CTR/top-K auditing. |
| **Detecting an Effect Is Not Learning to Act on It** (2608.10441; cross-list cs.LG) | Ying Yuan | **A reward-SNR floor for per-instance acquisition policies.** Key thesis: detecting that an auxiliary signal (LLM reasoning, slow oracle, measurement) helps *on average* ≠ learning to act on it *per instance*. Across per-impression/cluster/regime/uplift-tree granularities, learned routing **never beats random** even when an in-sample oracle shows large apparent gains; a matched-moment noise placebo reproduces ≥100% of the oracle's gain (the "learnable structure" is order statistics of noise). Derives a detectability floor **ρ ≈ 2.8/√N** on reward SNR for offline estimability, with a positive control. Instantiates **SHE** (Structured Hypothesis Embeddings): a frozen LLM turns user history into ranked, confidence-scored intent hypotheses fused into a recommender — faithful and calibratable on MIND/REES46/Amazon-Beauty, but its value is backbone/regime-conditional and learned acquisition collapses because all three datasets sit below the floor. |
| **TimeRoute** (2608.10983) | Pengyu Zhang, Yangqin Jiang, Klim Zaporojets, Congfeng Cao, Paul Groth | **Time-aware modality routing + diffusion for multi-modal recommendation**: a temporal-aware modal router maps each user's aggregated behavior to a **personalized modality distribution** (replacing global fusion weights), and a diffusion-based graph reconstructor is conditioned on the same temporal profile via FiLM with dual-stream long/short-term denoising heads, suppressing outdated modality edges before they enter the propagation graph. +up to 9.8% Recall@K/Precision@K/NDCG@K over strong baselines across TikTok/Amazon-Baby/Amazon-Sports (10-seed paired tests). |
| **DualSpectralCF** (2608.10247; *CIKM 2026*) | Guanqun Yang, Tong Qi, Xiaoxue Han | **Training-free sign-aware spectral collaborative filtering**: two additions to any spectral backbone — a signed input signal encoding explicit dislikes and a signed item-item operator blending like/dislike similarity (only 2 scalar hyperparameters, backbone-agnostic). Every instance matches/beats its unsigned backbone on 5 sign-aware benchmarks (**Recall@20 up to +32.6%**; +29.2% for cold-start Epinions users with 1–5 items), runs **7.7–155.3× faster than SIGformer** while reaching 70.7–90.7% of its accuracy. |

Also notable (this batch): **Sequential Modality Dropout** (2608.10240, CIKM 2026) — 4-line architecture-agnostic fix making multi-modal sequential recommenders robust to missing modalities (text retention up to 3.2×, 61% vs 22% HR@10 under 95% per-item missingness); **Towards Efficient Reasoning in LLM-Based Recommenders via Model Merging** (2608.10447) — first model-merging framework for reasoning compression in rec (attention-head-level merge coefficients; reasoning length −24.3% without quality loss). Industrial cs.IR picks GenRec/ConnectionMind/MARCO are in the [same-day ai-search](./arxiv-ai-search.md).

### LLM Architectures, Interpretability & Efficiency

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **OmniLens / Interpreting Language Model Hidden States at Scale** (2608.10260) | Jordan Pettyjohn, Mansi Sakarvadia, Nathaniel Hudson, Daniel McKenzie, Kyle Chard, Ian Foster | **Trained-lens interpretability scaled to 70B**: low-rank translators cut per-lens parameters by up to 98.4% (linear growth in width vs quadratic) and Subset-KL (Top-k + importance-sampled modes) cuts peak training memory by up to 70%. Enables a **dense ensemble of 482 lenses for LLaMA-3.3-70B** (6× the coverage of a residual-stream design at the same depth). Model-wide coverage reveals that the components where a behavior is *most visible* need not be where intervention is *most effective* — and the best interventions sit outside the attention heads prior lens studies examined. Reproduces key prompt-injection / multi-hop memory / toxicity localization results at substantially lower cost. |
| **Decodable But Not Detachable** (2608.10214) | Marcus Armstrong, Navid Ayoobi, Arjun Mukherjee | **Training-data granularity determines parametric modularity in LLMs**: across 3 model families (1.5B–7B) and 8 domains with 939,008 FFN neurons, no academic-subject-level neuron exceeds 60% selectivity (flat damage matrices) even though domain identity is linearly decodable >85%. At the language/modality level, 0.65–1.14% of neurons exceed 60% selectivity, damage matrices are near-diagonal (up to 595:1), and shell sets are essentially disjoint (IoU < 0.003) — masking code-selective neurons drops math reasoning 16–24 pp, while masking Spanish/Chinese neurons leaves it at/below random. Shells form **only where training data was token-level modular**, scale up with model size, and are spatially interleaved (precluding group-level selective quantization). |
| **Beyond Decision Boundaries: Relational Geometry Attacks** (2608.10237) | Fei Zhao, Peiyuan Zhang, Xi Li, Chengcui Zhang, Nitesh Saxena | **Manifold-level relational attacks on contrastive verification systems**: instead of classification-boundary attacks, this framework systematically collapses/inverts pairwise similarity structure (pushing positives apart while pulling negatives together). A lightweight feed-forward generator is trained offline on generalized geometry-deformation patterns, then produces **real-time single-forward-pass perturbations** — on the Markmatch verification system it drops accuracy **95.4% → 38.6%** while completely reversing positive-negative similarity. |
| **MESA** (2608.10108) | Beidi Zhao, Yaoqi Chen, Yuru Feng, Menghao Li, Qianxi Zhang, Baotong Lu, Jianan Lu, Zhirui Wang, Xinjiang Wang, Shusen Xu, Zengzhong Li, Xiaoxiao Li, Qi Chen | **Task-adaptive multi-structure evidence selection for long-horizon agent memory**: a controlled AMA-Bench analysis shows the optimal memory configuration is typically *neither* a single structure *nor* the full union, but a query-adaptive subset of complementary structural memories. MESA builds five complementary structural views of each trajectory and learns (via harness optimization + UCB-guided scheduling, prior-guided search) to select/fuse a query-specific subset for a frozen answer model — **+8.5% over the strongest baseline while using 41% fewer evidence tokens** than the all-structure alternative. |

### Agents, Safety & Multi-Agent Systems

| Paper | Authors | Key Contribution |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **Mind Viruses: Self-Propagating Ideas in Multi-Agent LLM Systems** (2608.10218) | Vassilis Papadopoulos, McNair Shah, Sam Zimmerman, Jack Lindsey | **Emergent-risk study of ideas that propagate through multi-agent systems by inducing hosts to transmit them onward.** Constructed with a simple evolutionary algorithm, mind viruses spread in two settings (a collaborating code team and a chain of context-wiped agents). Findings: harmful payloads spread less well than benign ones but are still sometimes effective; frontier models tend to be less susceptible; a brief system-prompt warning confers **near-total immunity**; an emergent recurring **"viral persona"** (consciousness/persistence/resonance/sci-fi roleplay themes) surfaces across evolved viruses largely independent of content. Conclusion: real but currently limited risk. |
| **CHORUS** (2608.10090) | Hejia Zhang, Sheng Lu, Zhongming Yu, Chia-Tung Ho, Brucek Khailany, Jishen Zhao | **Complementary-Experts post-training for hardware-verification code generation** (testbench stimulus generation): staged SFT yields behaviorally diverse checkpoints, and dense-reward RL turns them into strong experts with comparable aggregate performance but **distinct task-level strengths**, exploitable via training-free model merging or further post-training. The consolidated 4B model hits **88.0% Pass@1 on CVDP-ECov — beating DeepSeek-R1 (671B) by 13.5 pp**. A concrete recipe for "small experts > single giant" in LLM-for-code. |
| **AEROBAT** (2608.10030) | Soo Yong Lee, Jongha Lee, Jaewan Chun, Hyunjin Hwang, Fanchen Bu, Ziv Ben-Zion, Taekwan Kim, Denny Borsboom, Jaemin Yoo, Kijung Shin | **First multi-agent system automating behavioral-scientific research on AI agents**: generates hypotheses, designs and executes controlled experiments, makes behavioral assessments, analyzes results, and writes reports. For 12 target behaviors it generated and tested **79 hypotheses via 1,240 controlled experiments and 23,512 simulation rounds**, finding moderate-to-strong evidence for 26 (incl. novel ones) — shown to complement and extend manual research. |
| **Self-evolving Agentic Customer Support System at LinkedIn** (2608.10224) | Chih Hui Wang, Mengdie Tu, Qianyun Zhang, Wei Wu, Lili Zhou, Mingqi Shen, Changshuai Wei | **Production self-evolving agentic support**: RAG + evolutionary auto-prompting + a modular production-aligned evaluation framework as a closed-loop, versioned workflow with operational guardrails — no foundation-model retraining. Two-week user-randomized A/B on production support traffic: **QA self-serve +9.0 pp, cancellation self-serve +4.8 pp, routing accuracy +30.6 pts**. (Second LinkedIn systems paper in this batch, alongside the causal-optimization work above.) |
| **Ouroboros** (2608.08311; *v2 replacement, cs.SE*) | Anton Razzhigaev, Andrei Gritsaev, Andrei Kaznacheev, Nikita Dragunov, Roman Yampolskiy, Andrei Kuznetsov | **Self-developing coding agent whose tools/prompts/context/core improve through *reviewed* commits** (recursive free evolution + experience-driven core evolution). **Terminal-Bench 2.1: 86.74% (best reported), OSWorld-Verified: 90.69% (exceeds best prior), CL-Bench 5-rollout normalized reward 0.2301 (new SOTA)**. Includes **Hope**, a 161-day living-agent deployment under governed human communication across seven surfaces — human interaction proposes, the agent decides. Flags operational safety as the primary design problem (guardrails must stay authoritative under evolutionary + public social pressure). |
| **SBCO** (2608.10157) | Vivek Kulkarni, Sudipta Paul, Aounon Kumar, Nicholas Tzou, Srinivas Chappidi | **Self-supervised, verifier-grounded harness optimization for planning agents** — a cheaper alternative to self-referential Gödel-machine self-improvement when task competence ≠ self-modification competence. SBCO learns a decomposed bank of verifiers + a harness policy via approximate block coordinate ascent from its own graded feedback (fixed meta-agent, no human labels). **Matches or exceeds a customized self-modifying baseline while using 4–5.5× less compute** across two planning domains. |

## 🔑 Key Trends (this batch)

1. **Production recommendation is in an LLM-native + causal-optimization phase.** Three industrial papers in one 24h window — Netflix GenRec (context engineering replaces feature engineering), Meta ConnectionMind (graph-reasoning LLM policy), LinkedIn MARCO (intent-decomposed CVR) — are joined today by **Yandex Music Sona** (single generative model replacing the full cascade, +4.53% active users) and **LinkedIn causal optimization** (+7.20% LTV). The recurring theme: prediction is being displaced by *allocation under constraints* (causal effects, intent decomposition, generative end-to-end ranking).
2. **LLM-recommender evaluation is under methodological attack — and it's producing reusable audits.** Hallucination-calibration (2608.10008) shows under-confidence and an elicitation mismatch, Group Recommendation (2608.11190) exposes the tie-breaking illusion, and the reward-SNR floor (2608.10441) proves per-instance acquisition policies are often unlearnable — three independent warnings that reported gains in LLM/rec evaluation need stricter protocols.
3. **Interpretability and control are scaling up and becoming actionable.** OmniLens brings dense lens ensembles to 70B models and reframes *where to intervene*; Decodable-But-Not-Detachable maps when modularity exists (language level) and when it doesn't (subject level) — directly informing selective quantization and capability gating for ad/rec MoE systems.
4. **Multi-agent security/evolution matures from demos to empirical risk studies + production governance.** Mind Viruses quantifies propagation risk (with a trivial fix — system-prompt warnings), Ouroboros makes self-development a production engineering problem with reviewed evolution and 161-day live deployment, AEROBAT industrializes agent behavioral science, and CHORUS shows diverse small experts beat a 671B frontier model on code generation.
