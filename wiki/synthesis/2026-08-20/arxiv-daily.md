## Key Trends Observed

1. **Generative Recommendation Goes Production**: Multiple papers (GR4AD, OneRanker, GRAB, GenLI) demonstrate generative recommendation deployed at scale (Kuaishou, Tencent, Baidu, Xiaohongshu), achieving 1-4% revenue lifts.

2. **LLM Integration without Inference Cost**: A dominant pattern emerges — distill LLM knowledge at training time, then serve lightweight models (rEDMRec, IDProxy, Pre-trained LLMs Meet Sequential Recommenders). Zero LLM inference overhead at serving.

3. **Unified Generation-Ranking**: The boundary between retrieval, generation, and ranking continues to blur (OneRanker, GR4AD, GenCI), moving toward end-to-end architectures.

4. **Scaling via Architecture Innovation**: Loop scaling (LoopCTR), personalization tokens (PerSRec), and hybrid attention (HyTRec) offer new ways to scale without proportional parameter/compute increases.

5. **Frequency-Domain CTR**: FEDIN pioneers target-aware frequency analysis for CTR, discovering spectral entropy differences between positive/negative samples — a promising new direction.

6. **Industrial Deployment as Standard**: Nearly half the papers include production deployment results, reflecting the field's maturity and tight industry-academia feedback loops.
