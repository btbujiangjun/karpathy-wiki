## Recommendation / IR / Advertising

### 28. Adapting Generative Recommenders for Multi-Turn Interaction (INTEGER)
- **Authors**: Yu-Chen Den, Zhi Rui Tam, Yung-Yu Shih, Shih-Hsin Wang, Yun-Nung Chen, Pu-Jen Cheng, Eugene Yang
- **Subjects**: cs.IR / cs.AI
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08136
- **Key innovations**: INTEGER extends generative recommendation to multi-turn interaction via (1) a learned routing token that decides when to recommend, (2) history re-anchoring conditioning each item on both past behavior and dialogue, and (3) behavioral replay with instruction-data rehearsal to prevent forgetting. Improves Hit@10 by 13.3% on Amazon Beauty; matches/exceeds strongest baselines on Amazon Beauty and Toys.

### 29. Natural Language Questions as an Interface for Knowledge Graphs: QRAKEN Graph Distillation and Semantic Self-Healing
- **Authors**: Remo Grillo, Lukas Klic, Giovanni Colavizza
- **Subjects**: cs.AI
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08095
- **Key innovations**: QRAKEN — training-free, ontology-agnostic neurosymbolic Text-to-SPARQL pipeline grounded in empirical graph evidence (TTQL distillation + deterministic checks). Strict F1 0.643/0.652 on CK25 with GPT-4.1 mini / GPT-5.4 (~30–32% relative gains over strongest recomputed participant); TTQL dominates over shape-only/SHACL.

### 30. Evidence-Gated Research: Statistically Controlled Model Adoption in Adaptive Search
- **Authors**: Yifan Guo
- **Comments**: 17 pages, 4 figures. Preprint
- **Subjects**: cs.LG
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01751
- **Key innovations**: EGR — a statistical adoption layer for moving-incumbent search (freeze challengers, anytime-valid evidence, persistent candidate e-value, online controller) with FDR control; 56.1% less decision evidence in matched replay.
