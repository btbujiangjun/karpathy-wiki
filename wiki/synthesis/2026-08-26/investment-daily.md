# 2026-08-26 Daily Investment Briefing

## Key Events

1. **NVIDIA earnings today** — Q2 FY2027 (fiscal quarter ending July 2026) reported after market close. Guided Q3 revenue $91B ±2%, gross margin 75%. Last quarter Q1 revenue was $81.62B (+85% YoY). Consensus expects ~$93.5B revenue, EPS $2.13. The key narrative is Vera Rubin — investors want to know when it ships, not whether it sells. Stock has been flat since last report, slight dip last week.

2. **Alibaba $12.5B equity raise** — HK$80B ($10.2B USD) placement completed Aug 25. Funds earmarked for AI infrastructure. Stock dipped on dilution concern but pre-market bounced ~3% on 8/25. If AI spending delivers, dilution becomes irrelevant.

3. **XPeng robotics subsidiary IPO at $6.3B valuation** — Raised $900M for humanoid robots and embodied AI. First L4 autonomous Robotaxi rolled out Aug 10. Stock -7% on Q2 revenue miss — let the hype settle, entry opportunity emerging for those who believe in the long-term thesis.

4. **A-share chip rotation** — Semi chip stocks taking profits (存储, Optical Modules), AI hardware and application layer gaining traction. Leading H1 results: Zhongji Xuchuang 241.7% net profit, Cambricon +122.6% YoY.

5. **Shein HK IPO** — Targeting ~US$1.8B raise at ~US$27B valuation, book-build starts late Aug, trading expected early Sept. First day of trading Sept 1 (source: 金融界).
