## Cross-cutting themes

- **Deep-search / agentic RL is the densest cluster**: KnowGPO (knowledge-state credit), IG-calibrated OPD (evidence-aware distillation), TRACER (tool retrieval), SSR (agent discovery), CARA (causal graph retrieval). Recurring thesis: *credit/utility should be anchored to what was actually learned or retrieved, not to surface trajectory state.*
- **Long-horizon reliability**: goal-drift steering (`2610.22316`), FDE-Gym's discovery-of-unstated-constraints bottleneck (`2610.22350`), and evaluation-awareness in non-English (`2610.22333`) all probe where agents silently diverge from intent.
- **Architecture**: MoE routing exploration (MirrorMoE) and continuous diffusion-LM Transformers (LangDiT) are the two cleanest architecture contributions of the block.
- **World models / robotics**: CoDA, T2-z, GeoState, DSP — a strong test-time-adaptation and data-engine co-evolution showing.
