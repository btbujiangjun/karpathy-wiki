# ArXiv Paper Check - 2026-10-18

Quick scan of new submissions in AI and CTR-related areas from the last 24 hours.

## Selected Papers

### 1. Native Multimodal Representation Learning for Click-Through Rate Prediction in E-Commerce Scenarios
- **Authors**: Chao Yi, Feifan Yang, Jiawei Feng, Sishuo Chen, Zhangming Chan, Xiang-Rong Sheng, Han Zhu
- **ArXiv**: [2608.24091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24091)
- **Category**: cs.IR (Information Retrieval)
- **Key contribution**: Proposes Mine-Then-Train method to learn native multimodal representations aligned with user click preferences in CTR prediction, addressing misalignment between multimodal pretraining and CTR objectives.

### 2. IDProxy: Cold-Start CTR Prediction for Ads and Recommendation at Xiaohongshu with Multimodal LLMs
- **Authors**: Yubin Zhang, Haiming Xu, Guillaume Salha-Galvan, Ruiyan Han, Feiyang Xiao, Yanhua Huang, Li Lin, Yang Luo, Yao Hu
- **ArXiv**: [2603.01590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01590)
- **Category**: cs.IR
- **Key contribution**: Uses MLLMs to generate proxy ID embeddings from multimodal content for cold-start items in ads/recommendation, deployed at scale on Xiaohongshu.

### 3. Counterfactual Neighborhood Risk Fields (CNRF) for Selective VLM Safety
- **Authors**: Tianshi Wang, Jingsong Wang, Fengling Li, Jingjing Li, Xin Li, Lei Zhu
- **ArXiv**: [2610.20965](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20665) (from recent batch)
- **Category**: cs.AI / cs.CR / cs.MM
- **Key contribution**: Evidence-grounded selective safety for VLMs using counterfactual neighborhoods; separates unfamiliarity from harmfulness.

### 4. MLExpert: Towards a Generalist Agentic Model for Autonomous Machine Learning Engineering
- **Authors**: Shangheng Du, Jinxin Shi, Shuaiyu Zhang, Y. Wu, Junpeng Zhao, Shiyang Feng, Xin Li, Liang He, Xiangchao Yan, Bo Zhang
- **ArXiv**: [2610.22344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22344)
- **Category**: cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.LG
- **Key contribution**: Generalist agentic model for autonomous ML engineering tasks.

### 5. KnowGPO: Knowledge-Grouped Policy Optimization for Search Agents
- **Authors**: Siqi Chen, Zhengxi Lu, Yuchen Yan, Xingyu Wu, Qipeng Chen, Xin Zhang, Aiting Liu, Chao Deng, Jie Liu, Jin Ma, Weiming Lu, Jun Xiao, Yongliang Shen
- **ArXiv**: [2610.22331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22331)
- **Category**: cs.AI
- **Key contribution**: Knowledge-grouped policy optimization improving search agent behavior.
